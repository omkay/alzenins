## The spike found a real error in our own assumptions

`AGENTS.md` §3.6 says don't guess integration behaviour. It paid off in minutes.

**MamoPay accepts twelve currencies, not the twenty-eight its marketing pages
claim.** The sandbox rejects anything else with
`VALIDATION_ERROR: Currency must be AED, EUR, GBP, RON, SAR, TRY, USD, AUD, CAD,
CHF, DZD, EGP`.

**QAR is not on that list**, and ADR-0003 had Qatar in the covered column. Nine
of sixteen markets now need a fallback, not eight. Qatar is charged in AED —
both currencies are dollar-pegged, so the number stays stable.

Had the spike not run first, Qatar would have shipped with an authored QAR price
that failed at the payment step: a market silently unable to buy.

Open with Mamo, and needed **in writing before the price books are authored**:
is twelve a sandbox restriction or the real list?

## Other observed facts

Full detail in `docs/spikes/mamopay-sandbox.md`. The ones that shape the adapter:

- **Amounts are decimal floats** (`390.0`), not minor units. Our tables store
  bigint minor units deliberately. The adapter converts at the boundary and
  never lets a float back in.
- `external_id` is echoed back — the correlation key to our order.
- `custom_data` round-trips arbitrary JSON intact.
- **`lang: "ar"` is accepted** — the hosted checkout can be Arabic, which matters
  a great deal for this audience.
- `capacity: 1` makes a link single-use, which is what a one-off order wants.
- `id` (`MB-LINK-…`) is the provider ref; `payment_url` is the redirect target.
- Errors carry a branchable `error_code` plus human-readable `errors[]`.
- Lists paginate via a `pagination_meta` envelope, not headers.

## One of my own tests was wrong

The staging suite asserted `/` always lands on `/ar`. It does not — next-intl
negotiates from `Accept-Language`, and an earlier curl check only suggested
otherwise because curl sends no such header. Both directions are now asserted.

Worth a product decision at some point: many Gulf students run English-locale
devices, so an Arabic-first school currently sends a good share of its audience
to the English site by default. Not changing routing unilaterally.
