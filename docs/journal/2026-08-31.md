## Learned

The full detail is in the spike. The three that change what we build:

1. **Webhooks are not signed.** No HMAC, no timestamp, no event id. Mamo sends a
   static `auth_header` we choose, in the `Authorization` header. Verification is
   a constant-time compare; deliveries are replayable; the body proves nothing.
   Rule that follows: on `charge.succeeded`, **re-fetch the charge by id** and
   grant from that, never from the webhook body.
2. **Unknown fields are dropped silently with a 201.** `externalId` instead of
   `external_id` produces a live payment link with no correlation key and no
   error. Every create must assert its own echo.
3. **Termly subscriptions work** — not as a frequency, but as
   `frequency: monthly, frequency_interval: 3`. And `payment_quantity: 6` gives a
   bounded run of charges, which is the honest shape for "6 months prepaid".
