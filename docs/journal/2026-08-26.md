  **390 AED**. Worth reconciling before the price sheet is written.

**MamoPay (from Mamo's own docs):**
- Supports payment links, **subscription links**, payouts, and webhooks via API.
- Sandbox: `https://sandbox.dev.business.mamopay.com`, log in with the live account email
  + OTP. Account verification is required first.
- Webhook events cover payment success/failure and **recurring** payment success/failure.
- **28 charge currencies**, including AED, SAR, QAR, EGP, DZD, USD, EUR, GBP.
- **Settlement is AED to a UAE bank account.** Multi-currency settlement is "coming soon".
- Customers paying in their own currency avoid their bank's FX fee, but **the business
  absorbs Mamo's FX cost**.
- **The gap that shapes the architecture:** MamoPay does **not** support JPY, KWD, BHD,
  OMR, JOD, IQD, MAD, or ILS. That is 8 of the 16 target markets, including **Japan** —
  the most important one for a Japanese-language school. This is why the `PaymentProvider`
  port exists from day one rather than being a later refactor.

## Learned

- **Tailwind v4 `@theme` keys can silently collide with next/font variables.**
  Declaring `--font-arabic: var(--font-arabic), …` inside `@theme inline` is a
  circular reference — it resolves to nothing and the font silently falls back.
  Symptom: the Arabic ز logo mark rendered as a mangled glyph on the English
  side. Fix: give next/font its own variable names (`--font-cairo`) and have the
  theme key point at those.
- **`dir="ltr"` on a block element also changes its text alignment.** `400+` was
  reordering to `+400` under RTL; putting `dir="ltr"` on the block fixed the
  digits but dragged the number to the left margin. It has to go on an inline
  element inside the block. Worth remembering for every price and date we render.
- Next 16 deprecates the `middleware.ts` filename in favour of `proxy.ts`; the
  export shape is unchanged, so it is a straight rename.
- next-intl's `router.replace` only accepts an object with `params` when the
  routing config declares dynamic pathnames. With plain string paths, pass the
  pathname directly.
- pnpm 10 blocks postinstall scripts by default; `onlyBuiltDependencies` belongs
  in `pnpm-workspace.yaml`, not `package.json`.
